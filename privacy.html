Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Your privacy comes first

Bookshelf is local-first and privacy-first. We do not collect, upload or sell your personal data. There is no account system and no analytics.

Data stays on your device

  • Book files and cover images are stored only in the App’s sandbox.
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings are kept on this device.
  • Reading time is recorded on this device only.
  • You can export and restore a free local backup at any time. Automatic backup, when turned on, keeps only the last 2 copies on this device. Deleting the App removes all of that data.
  • We never upload your files. iOS device backups (iCloud/Finder) are managed by the system and may include app data.

Face ID

Face ID is used only on-device to unlock hidden books and for the optional App Lock. Biometric data is handled by iOS; the App never receives or stores it.

Wi-Fi transfer

While the transfer screen is open, Wi-Fi import runs a temporary server on the local Wi-Fi network. It is protected by a one-time code and is discoverable on the local network by this device’s name (Bonjour). Files go from the browser to this device and are not uploaded to us.

No third-party SDKs

The App includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-login SDKs. It reads books on your device to display and search them, and does not upload that text. It uses Apple frameworks such as StoreKit and Face ID. It does not use push or local notifications.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, and you can restore purchases at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

To remove everything, delete the App from your device. Local books, progress and settings are removed with it.
