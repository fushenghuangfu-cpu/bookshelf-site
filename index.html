Support

A few answers are below. You can also email us.

Importing

Use the file picker in the library. Novels can be TXT, EPUB, or FB2. Comics can be CBZ, ZIP, CBR, RAR, PDF, or loose images. Audiobooks can be MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, CAF, or FLAC. Files stay on this device.

Wi-Fi transfer

While the transfer screen is open, Bookshelf runs a temporary server on the local Wi-Fi network. A one-time code protects uploads, and the device is discoverable on the local network by its name (Bonjour). Files go from the browser to this device and are not uploaded to us.

Restore Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. In Settings, tap Restore Purchase to bring a previous purchase onto this device.

How are PDFs read?

PDFs are imported as comic pages and read as page images, not as reflowable text.

Deleting data

Delete the app from your device to remove everything. Local books, progress, and settings are removed with it. There is no account to close.
